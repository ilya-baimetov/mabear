Deterministic End-to-End Story Pipeline

Every paragraph is compiled into a deterministic, seekable Timeline. Physics continuity runs upfront in Rapier3D and reconciles landing coordinates back into subsequent actions.

01 · AUTHOR

Story Text / Remix

User enters English or Russian prose in MomentWorkbench or FullStoryDialog, or picks a preset episode.

02 · DIRECT

Offline / LLM Director

offline/parser.ts resolves verbs, carrots, kitchen containers & locations deterministically; /api/direct handles open-ended LLM BeatScripts.

03 · COMPILE

Validate & Stage

compile.ts validates props & cast, routes around yard obstacles, schedules interactions, camera framing, lighting, and audio cues.

04 · SIMULATE

Rapier3D Continuity

rapierReplay.ts solves ball rolls, drops, and obstacle paths using active-window fast-forward, updating Trajectory.prepareActions.

05 · EVALUATE

Pure evaluate(t)

evaluate.ts & kinematics.ts compute exact actor transforms, facial rigs, and prop poses with WeakMap track caching.

06 · RENDER

WebGL2 + WebAudio

Stage renders batched static sets + 1-pass baked characters; Player drops to 0 FPS when paused and settled.
