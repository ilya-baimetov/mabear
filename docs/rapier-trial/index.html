What the result supports

Continue with selective integration. Static obstacles and loose objects benefit. Whole-character physics is not justified by this trial.

Only the covered central yard, Masha’s capsule and one ball are simulated. Hugs, rides, pickups, other characters and locations retain animation ownership. Blocked movement changes the destination reached; the compiler does not yet replan subsequent interaction marks. Later pickup of a settled ball still uses its semantic mark. Actor/ball collision is excluded. These are rollout blockers, not completed features.

The Rapier chunk adds about 1.67 MB compressed, loaded by the demo or explicit editor flag. Long uncached seeks still need background precomputation before wider use. Stories over 180 seconds fall back to animation.
