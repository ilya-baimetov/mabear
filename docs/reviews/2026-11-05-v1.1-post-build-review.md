- A1 [partial]: The main path is correct and tested. These gaps remain, ranked by demo impact:

(1) HIGH — The stage freezes during the hold when nothing has rendered yet. player.ts:107-108 runs idle motion only when timeline.duration > 0 and t ≥ duration. Otherwise evalT stays at t, which is 0 here. After ⟲ New + ▶ (DEMO_SCRIPT D3, the "magic" moment), characters, wind grass, smoke and bees all hold still for the whole ~7 s Opus wait under a pulsing "Getting ready…". The same happens on a card ▶ while the player sits at t=0, e.g. an example loaded but not yet played. NOTES.md claims "idle motion keeps going". Fix: advance the idle clock whenever the player is not playing (for duration 0, or while store.waiting), or drive ambient animation from wall time.

(2) MED — Tapping a character during the hold corrupts the resume point. Stage.tsx:162-170 accepts taps whenever !player.isPlaying; the hint pill is hidden but taps still work. When the reaction stops, playback.onPlayerStopped(reactionEnd) sets holdAt to the old duration plus ~3 s. When the next paragraph arrives, start(holdAt) skips its first ~3 s. If the paragraph is shorter than that, it never plays, because tick() ends the session. Kids will tap while waiting. Fix: ignore taps while a session is waiting, or have onPlayerStopped ignore stops on a reaction timeline or clamp holdAt to the store's timeline duration.

(3) MED — A director call can fire without ▶, on half-typed text. Every director reply calls recompile({redirectBroken:"direct"}) (store.ts:595-613), including replies that land after Pause or seek has ended the session, or while the user is typing. Its microtask calls prepare(idx), which directs every dirty paragraph up to idx, not just the broken one. The paragraph being edited flips to ⏳, is sent mid-typing, and the timeline is cut off at it. It needs a compile error to trigger (a prop held by another actor, compile.ts:297-313). The Wolves few-shot makes this likelier, because it leaves wolf_a holding honey_pot. Fix: re-direct only the broken paragraph with a single direct() call against its real start, and only while a playback session is active. Otherwise use "mark".
