## Code-only implementation cautions

These are dependencies for a future revision, **not additional observed failures**:

- `src/engine/set/forestLocations.ts` deliberately keeps the immediate actor/van area flat; its hills begin outside that area. Introducing a local slope requires coordinated actor/vehicle grounding, not merely moving the decorative terrain.
- Pond geometry, navigation and fishing marks are coupled through `src/shared/pond.ts` and `src/engine/set/bearsYard.ts`. A larger bank or moved bench/pier needs matching contact and navigation updates; a visually revised water mesh alone is insufficient.

The house facade was inspected, but the saved exterior shots are too close to justify a precise whole-house reconstruction claim. Resolve that silhouette with a suitable wider source view if it becomes a separate priority. My recommendation is to start with **one complete fishing scene**: it exposes camera, set layout, prop continuity and vegetation together, and gives a concrete pass/fail result before broader asset work.
