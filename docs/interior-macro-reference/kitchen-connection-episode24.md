## Observed versus inferred topology

| Relationship | Status | Blockout implication |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen counter/window/stove sequence wraps rather than forming one flat furniture row | Observed in episode 17, 5:40 | Give the kitchen its own depth and angled wall returns. |
| Cooking wall connects openly beside the fireplace side of the living area | Strong inference from both zones visible together in episode 24, 1:52.8 | Use an open connection; do not invent a dedicated kitchen door. |
| Fireplace/gallery → chair/bookcase → plaid curtain | Observed local adjacencies across episodes 17 and 24 | Preserve this wrapping sequence; the curtain is not opposite the bookcase. |
| Plaid curtain is the kitchen entrance | Unverified | Do not label it as kitchen access. |
| Exact wall angles, dimensions and complete floor-plan loop | Unverified | Keep these provisional; the preparation table may be movable staging. Fixed fixtures are stronger anchors. |

## Coverage boundary

Episode 24 was inspected in the actual player at 1:07.8, 1:12.8, 1:17.8, 1:22.8, 1:32.8 and 1:52.8; the two decisive frames were saved. The 1:32.8 reverse view adds a window and refrigerator edge. Episode 26 samples at 1:20, 2:10 and 5:44 showed renovation/reading-room views without another kitchen connection. The four episode 17 frames above were also inspected. This is bounded sampling, not a complete-episode or exact architectural reconstruction claim.
